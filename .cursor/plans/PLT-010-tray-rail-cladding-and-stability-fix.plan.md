## Finding 2 — upper-tray tip factor 1.300 vs 1.5 floor

**Root cause (verified from `analysis.py::stability_report_inputs`, lines 256-296):** the current model computes `factor = restore_arm / overturn_arm` where `restore_arm = case.depth/2 - foot_inset` (**same value for both tiers — mass-distribution-independent, footprint-only**) and `overturn_arm = extension/2` (125 mm lower, 200 mm upper). **`total_mass` appears identically in both the numerator and denominator and cancels exactly** — this is why lower (2.080) vs upper (1.300) differ *only* by the extension ratio (400/250=1.6, and 1.3×(400/250)=... check: 2.080 × 250/400 = 1.300, confirms exact algebraic identity) and why **added ballast mass changes nothing under the current formula, regardless of amount** — a genuine modelling flaw, not just an unlucky footprint. Also: the model's `pivot_edge` string says "front foot line" for lower but "rear foot line" for upper — this is inconsistent with `kinematics.py::apply_tray_extension` (`dy = -extension_mm` for **both** `LOWER_KINEMATIC_GROUP` and `UPPER_KINEMATIC_GROUP` — both tiers slide toward the front/Y=0, per D-033 aligned front Y) and should be corrected to "front foot line" for both as part of this fix.

Real levers checked and ruled out:
- **Foot stance:** `hardware.py::build_feet` already places feet with `inset = diameter/2` — center exactly `radius` in from each true corner, i.e. **already the maximum possible splay** for a 30 mm foot (`hardware.foot_diameter_mm`, TZ:300 floor — cannot shrink further). No stance-widening lever available.
- **Extension:** fixed at 400 mm minimum (TZ:179) — not touched.

**Required model correction (implementer, same rigor as `deflection_report.md`):** replace the mass-cancelling ratio with a **split stationary/moving mass** static moment model that correctly credits which mass actually shifts forward with the extended tray, instead of (incorrectly) applying the whole system mass to both arms:

- Pivot: front foot line, `pivot_y_mm = foot_inset_mm` (unchanged formula, `hardware.foot_diameter_mm/2`).
- `moving_mass_kg` = the extended tier's own modelled mass only: `part_mass_kg(TRAY-{LOWER,UPPER}-001)` + `params.plotter_mass_kg(1 or 2)` (slides/softstops/vibmounts stay `verify_on_real_machine`/zero-mass, unchanged convention — do not invent hardware mass).
- `moving_cog_y_rest_mm` = mass-weighted Y centroid of that tray panel + that plotter at rest (reuse `_solid_centroid_mm`/`plotter_mass_samples`-style geometry).
- `moving_cog_y_extended_mm = moving_cog_y_rest_mm - extension_mm` (sign matches `apply_tray_extension`).
- `stationary_mass_kg` = `empty_case_mass_kg(parts, params)` (the **real** computed structural total — do not use `mass_targets.empty_case_target_max_kg`, a paper ceiling, once mass no longer cancels; using an inflated ceiling here would overstate stability) **+** `params.plotter_mass_kg(other tier)` (the tier that stays retracted) **−** the moving tray panel's own mass (already inside `empty_case_mass_kg`, would double-count).
- `stationary_cog_y_mm` = mass-weighted Y centroid of everything else (`structural_mass_samples(parts, params)` minus the moving tray panel's sample, plus the other plotter's rest-position point mass).
- `restore_arm_mm = stationary_cog_y_mm - pivot_y_mm`; `overturn_arm_mm = pivot_y_mm - moving_cog_y_extended_mm` (positive since the extended CoG sits well in front of the pivot).
- `restore_moment = stationary_mass_kg * 9.80665 * restore_arm_mm`; `overturn_moment = moving_mass_kg * 9.80665 * overturn_arm_mm`; `factor = restore_moment/overturn_moment`.

A rough hand-check with rev9's own numbers (structural 7.023 kg, plotter1 4.7 kg, plotter2 5.2 kg, foot_inset 15 mm, rev9 CoM-at-rest y=196.3 mm) puts the corrected **upper** factor in the **~1.5-1.6** range and the **lower** factor comfortably higher (~3+) — i.e. this is a real, non-forced improvement from fixing the mass-cancellation flaw, not a foregone certainty. **The implementer must compute this precisely from live geometry, not from this estimate.**

**If the corrected model alone reaches ≥1.5:** report it as the fix, no ballast needed, keep `mass_targets.empty_case_target_max_kg` unused/removed from the stability path (still fine elsewhere), and skip ballast entirely.

**If it falls short:** add a real ballast part (e.g. `BALLAST-BASE-001`), positioned at the base, biased as far **rear** (large Y) as the footprint allows (maximizes `restore_arm_mm` without affecting `overturn_arm_mm`, since ballast does not move with either tray), sized to close the gap within the ~5 kg headroom (structural 7.023 kg → 12 kg ceiling). Use a real, sourceable density (e.g. mild steel ≈7850 kg/m³, a standard physical constant — mark `provenance: verified` with a citation, or `assumed` with a new `ASM-016`-class entry if the exact ballast product is undecided) added to `config/parameters.yaml` under a new leaf (e.g. `hardware.ballast_*` or `mass_targets.ballast_*`) — never invented without a traceable density source. Recompute the factor with ballast included in `stationary_mass_kg`/`stationary_cog_y_mm`.

**If ≥1.5 is still not reachable within the fixed decisions:** report the best honest achieved figure with full derivation — do not force it. This stays consistent with `docs/10_USER_INPUT_REQUIRED.md` §J's existing framing.

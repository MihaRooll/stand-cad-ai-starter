# Equipment measurement and evidence guide

## Coordinate convention

Define each device in its normal operating orientation:

- X: left to right;
- Y: front to rear;
- Z: support plane upward.

Record where the origin is and never change it silently between revisions.

## Capture more than the body box

For each device capture separate envelopes:

1. **Body:** physical closed device.
2. **Feet/support:** actual contact points and required flatness/levelling.
3. **Operating movement:** press arm, lid, trays, paper path, cutter carriage, doors.
4. **Loading:** paper, film, garment, cartridge, waste, tools, consumables.
5. **Service/removal:** hands, fasteners, access panel, full extraction path.
6. **Thermal/air:** intake, exhaust, hot surfaces, keep-clear distance.
7. **Cables:** connector body, plug, strain relief, bend radius, disconnect access.
8. **Transport restraint:** safe contact areas, straps/brackets, prohibited loads/orientation.

## Measurement method

- Use a rigid tape/caliper appropriate to size.
- Measure three times when a dimension is fit-critical.
- State uncertainty/resolution.
- Photograph the measurement setup.
- Do not infer feet or connector positions from overall dimensions.
- For a moving feature, record intermediate collision risk as well as end positions.
- Weigh the actual configured device where transport mass is critical; record installed consumables.

## Provenance fields

Every critical fact should include:

- value and units;
- source type: official document, exact web listing, user measurement, manufacturer response, assumption;
- source reference/file/photo;
- exact model/revision;
- measurement date;
- verifier;
- status: `UNVERIFIED`, `VERIFIED`, or `SUPERSEDED`.

# Consolidated user input required

Opus 5 must keep this as one consolidated list. Ask only for unresolved items that block the active gate.

## A. Select the equipment inside this one stand

For each item, mark quantity and whether it must:

- operate while installed;
- deploy from the stand;
- travel restrained inside;
- be removable without tools;
- be stored elsewhere.

Candidate inventory:

- Bulros T-330 or T-300 — select exact model;
- Epson L11050 or L1800 — select exact model;
- DS-RX1HS photo printer;
- Silhouette Cameo — provide exact generation/model;
- laptop and charger;
- iPad(s) and charger(s);
- power distribution/UPS/stabilizer;
- consumables, tools, waste, paper/film, cables.

## B. Evidence for every selected item

- photo of manufacturer/model label;
- manual or official product link/PDF;
- user measurements: width, depth, height, and mass if available;
- feet/support-point positions;
- photos from front/back/left/right/top/bottom with ruler or known reference;
- doors, trays, lids, arms, press, rolls, and connectors shown fully open/extended;
- ventilation openings and required clearance;
- cable/plug protrusion;
- loading, consumable change, cleaning, and removal path;
- transport/tilt/levelling restrictions.

## C. Stand operating scenario

- exact sequence from arrival to ready-for-work;
- number of people moving and deploying it;
- working height preference;
- devices used simultaneously;
- items accessed most often;
- acceptable setup and pack-down time;
- indoor/outdoor use, ambient temperature, dust, humidity, and public access;
- whether the operator or visitors can touch the stand.

## D. Transport and site constraints

- vehicle and usable cargo opening/interior dimensions;
- smallest doorway, lift, turn, corridor, threshold, and ramp;
- maximum acceptable total mass;
- lifting/loading method and available handles/ramps;
- storage footprint and orientation;
- floor type and unevenness;
- stairs: allowed or prohibited.

## E. Appearance and commercial constraints

- preferred reference: existing yellow/black concept, orange/grey concept, or new direction;
- brand colours/finish and cosmetic expectations;
- target budget range for one prototype;
- required completion date;
- desired service life and expected event frequency;
- whether future device swaps/modularity are required.

## F. Manufacturing choice

This can remain open through concept phase. Before Gate G5 the user must authorize which vendors may receive the RFQ package and approve the selected prototype quotation. Agents may prepare comparison material but may not contact or order without authorization.
